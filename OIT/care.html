Flower Care Guide

Six simple habits that make your bouquet last twice as long.

6 Simple Steps

1

Cut stems at an angle

Trim 2–3 cm at 45° under cool running water. A wider cut surface drinks more and never sits flat on the vase bottom.

2

Change the water daily

Fresh, cool water keeps bacteria down. Top up every day and replace it completely every two days.

3

Clean the vase

Wash the vase with warm soapy water before use. Bacteria are the main reason flowers fade early.

4

Strip the lower leaves

Leaves under the waterline rot quickly and cloud the water. Remove them before arranging.

5

Use flower food

The sachet that comes with your bouquet feeds the blooms and slows bacteria. Dissolve it fully.

6

Choose the right spot

Keep flowers cool, out of direct sun, and away from ripening fruit and heaters.
