Your Guide to Flower Shopping

Learn how to select the freshest flowers, choose the perfect bouquet, and care for your blooms to make them last.

What to Look for When Buying Flowers

Fresh Petals

Check the petals for firmness and vibrant color. Avoid flowers with bruised, wilted, or translucent petals. The bloom should feel crisp to the touch, not soft or papery.

Healthy Stems

Look for strong, straight stems without slimy or dark patches. Stems should be a vibrant green color. Avoid flowers with bent stems or signs of decay at the base.

Tight Buds

Flowers with closed or semi-open buds will last longer than fully bloomed flowers. This gives you more days to enjoy your bouquet as the flowers open naturally at home.

Fresh Foliage

The leaves should be green and turgid, not yellow or drooping. Remove any leaves that will sit below the waterline in your vase to prevent bacterial growth.

Choosing the Perfect Bouquet

Consider the Occasion

  • 🎊 Celebrations: Bright and bold colors like reds, oranges, and yellows bring energy and joy.
  • 💕 Romance: Classic roses in deep red or soft pink express love and devotion.
  • 😔 Sympathy: White and pale pink flowers convey respect and comfort during difficult times.
  • 🌈 Friendship: Mixed colors and cheerful varieties celebrate your bond and bring a smile.

Mix and Match

Create visual interest by combining:

  • ✓ Different sizes: Large focal flowers with smaller filler blooms
  • ✓ Varying textures: Smooth petals with ruffled or spiky varieties
  • ✓ Color harmony: Complementary colors or monochromatic shades
  • ✓ Greenery: Add eucalyptus or ferns for depth and elegance
  • ✓ Seasonal blooms: Mix in seasonal varieties for freshness and value

Smart Shopping Tips

⏰

Shop Early

Visit in the morning or early afternoon to get the freshest selection. Flowers that arrive early in the day have been in water longer.

❄️

Ask About Freshness

Don't hesitate to ask the florist when the flowers arrived. The fresher they are, the longer they'll last in your home.

💧

Ask About Conditioning

Request that your florist add flower food and condition the stems properly before wrapping your bouquet.

🌡️

Keep Them Cool

Transport your flowers in a cool environment. Keep them out of direct sunlight and away from hot cars.

📅

Plan Ahead

Order in advance for special occasions. This ensures availability and allows florists to prepare custom arrangements.

🤝

Build a Relationship

Regular customers often get preferred access to the best flowers and personalized recommendations for their needs.

Care When You Get Home

Remove plastic or paper wrapping immediately as it traps moisture and can cause petal rot. Let your flowers breathe for a few minutes before arranging.

Cut 2-3 cm off the bottom of each stem at a 45-degree angle with a sharp knife. Remove any foliage that would sit below the waterline in your vase.

Wash your vase thoroughly with warm soapy water and rinse completely. Use cool, clean water and add flower food if provided with your bouquet.

Place larger focal flowers first, then fill gaps with smaller blooms and greenery. Arrange them at different heights for a natural, full look.

Place your bouquet in a cool spot, away from direct sunlight, heating vents, and ripening fruit. Change the water every 2 days and re-trim stems weekly.
💐

With proper care, your flowers will stay fresh and vibrant for 7-14 days!
